## 活动介绍
Join us in person for an amazing story about adventure, passion, and determination! Hear firsthand how a public school teacher turned a dream into reality by traveling to every single country on the planet. Get inspired, ask questions, and discover the secrets behind this epic journey. Don’t miss out on this unique event packed with fun tales and cool travel tips!

Born and raised in San Jose, California, Lucy is a public school teacher who has fulfilled her dream of the ultimate travel goal: visiting every country in the world.

She got hooked on points and miles two decades ago, using them to fund her global humanitarian efforts and stretch her modest teacher’s salary.

With a deep love for Geography and World History, even as a young child Lucy developed a desire for travel, though she did not get her first passport until she was 23 years old. With 3 months of school holidays per year to explore the world, she spent many summers teaching and volunteering abroad, and finding creative ways to afford international travel on a budget.

She entered Syria in May 2023, becoming one of the first women to visit every country. In addition, she has run a marathon in all 50 states and all 7 continents.

Lucy gives talks all over the world, sharing her insight on juggling time, resources, and money in order to live a balanced and successful life: To her, that means excelling not only in travel, but in maintaining a career, family, physical fitness, and spiritual purpose.

## 关于我们

About us:

ValleyRain International, a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit cultural organization, was founded in March 2015 in the San Francisco Bay Area.

We were founded with the mission of building a bridge between Aisa and North America cultures. 

To date, we have hosted upwards of 500 cultural events in San Francisco, Los Angeles, Dallas，Beijing, and Shanghai, among other cities. At these events, we gather leading voices in the field to engage in dialogue and share knowledge.
